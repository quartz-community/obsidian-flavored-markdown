`foldHeadings` and `foldLists` options (both off by default): fold sections at their headings and list items with a sublist, as Obsidian's reading view does with "Fold heading" and "Fold indent". An arrow before the heading or item, shown on hover (always on touch screens), folds it; Obsidian themes style the arrows as in the app. Uses the fold arrows of `@quartz-community/rehype-obsidian`'s `collapseIndicators`.
