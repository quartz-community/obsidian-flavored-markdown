Render callouts and transclusions as `<div>` (as Obsidian does) and add Obsidian's classes (`tag` on tags, `internal-embed markdown-embed` on transclusions) so Obsidian themes apply. Default highlight/tag/embed styles move to the `quartz-base` layer so themes can override them.
